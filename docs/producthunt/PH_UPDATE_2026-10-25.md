## First maker comment (paste after saving)

```text
Hi Product Hunt — small but important correction to how we describe Marma-Crypto.

It is a tamper-evident seal for signed documents, not another encryption library and not a replacement for DocuSign/Adobe or a VDR. A visible geometric seal is bound to an exact cryptographic digest, so a lawyer or deal-team member can check the rendered page, not just the signature bytes. The production CLI handles PDF/DOCX/SVG; the browser demo is a zero-upload PDF/SVG preview using the legacy 256-bit carrier.

What it does well: visible tamper evidence, client-side verification, recipient canaries for vector-preserved copies, and a complement to PAdES/eIDAS/DSTU 4145. What it does not do: it does not trace screenshots, photos or printouts — those need a separate robust watermark layer — and it is not a notarization or VDR product.

We are looking for 3–5 legal/M&A teams for a paid pilot: one evidence set, a verification report, and a real go/no-go decision. If that is your workflow, the 15-minute evidence audit link is on the site. Feedback from lawyers and forensic document examiners is especially welcome.
```
