# Buyer interview script — legal/M&A evidence (10 calls)

**Goal:** find the last real incident and the current workaround, not feature requests.
**Target:** 5 law firms/M&A counsel, 3 notaries/forensic document examiners, 2 VDR/DMS product leads.
**Time:** 25–30 min. Record only with permission; anonymise.

## Opening (2 min)

"I'm researching how legal and M&A teams prove that a signed document they are looking at is the
document that was signed. I'm not selling in this call; I want to understand your workflow and where
it breaks. May I ask about the last real incident?"

## Questions

1. Walk me through the last time a signed document's visible content or origin was questioned.
   What happened, who was involved, and how was it resolved?
2. What did you do to check it? Signature validation, PDF metadata, comparing hashes, calling the
   counterparty, something else?
3. Where did the document live: email, VDR, DMS, court portal, shared drive? Who could download,
   print or screenshot it?
4. When a copy leaks or is altered, what evidence do you need to escalate internally or to a court?
5. Have you ever used document watermarking/DRM? What did you like or hate?
6. Who owns the budget for document security/evidence tooling? Who would approve a pilot?
7. What would a successful pilot prove in 2 weeks? What would make you say no?
8. What is the cost of one bad leak/tamper incident (time, fees, reputational, deal impact)?
9. Would you pay a fixed fee for a 2-week pilot with 10 documents and a verification report?
   What range feels reasonable?
10. May I show you a 5-minute demo and follow up with a pilot one-pager?

## Close

- Ask for one referral to a colleague with the same problem.
- Log: company type, role, incident, current workaround, budget owner, next step, follow-up date.

## Qualification signals

- Has had a real incident or a near-miss in the last 12 months.
- Uses a VDR/DMS for signed documents.
- Can name a budget owner and a decision date.
- Cares about visible evidence, not just signature validity.
- Accepts a paid pilot rather than asking for a free custom build.

## Disqualifiers

- Wants a full VDR/e-signature replacement.
- Only wants screenshot/photo leak tracing and refuses a robust layer.
- No document workflow with signed copies.
- "Send me a proposal" without a discovery call.
