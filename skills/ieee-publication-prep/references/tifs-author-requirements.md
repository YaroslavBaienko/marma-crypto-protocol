# TIFS author requirements — working profile

Verify against the current IEEE TIFS / SPS "Information for Authors" before every submission; these
values were checked for the 2026 submission cycle and are a starting point, not a substitute for the
live guidelines.

## Manuscript type and length

- **Regular Paper**.
- Initial submission: **≤13 double-column pages**, including appendices, references and biographies.
- Revised submission: **≤16 double-column pages**.
- Overlength page charges are mandatory above the published threshold.

## Format

- IEEE Transactions two-column, 10 pt, official IEEEtran class.
- Margins: the SPS author guidance states **≥1 inch**; standard IEEEtran journal margins are narrower.
  Build a 1-inch-margin alternate if needed and record which PDF was uploaded.
- Embed all fonts; no missing references/`??`; figures legible at column width.

## Abstract and index terms

- Abstract **150–250 words**, self-contained, no citations, no displayed equations.
- Index terms/author keywords are required.

## EDICS and subject categories

TIFS uses a hierarchical EDICS taxonomy; do not rely on legacy numeric codes. Current portal leaves
used for this line:

- `IF-WAT` Watermarking and data hiding; leaf `IF-WAT-MODL` Theoretical models.
- `IF-FOR-AUTH` Multimedia content authentication and plagiarism detection.
- Subject categories: **Watermarking and Data Hiding**; **Multimedia Forensics**.
- Do not select `IF-CRY` applied cryptography unless the contribution really is a cryptographic
  primitive; TIFS rejects pure-crypto papers that are not applied to a security problem.

## Author identity

- ORCID is required for every author and must match the manuscript, cover letter and portal profile.
- Single-anonymized review; author names/affiliations are visible to reviewers.
- CRediT roles: at least one per author; a sole author can claim conceptualization, methodology,
  software, validation, formal analysis, investigation, resources, data curation, writing – original
  draft, writing – review & editing, visualization and project administration. Do not claim funding
  acquisition or supervision unless true.

## Preprints and prior work

- Identical preprints/public author copies: list them at submission, but do not add them to the
  reference list.
- Other posted preprints: prior art; cite and explain the relationship.
- Prior rejection/resubmission: answer truthfully; provide venue/manuscript IDs and a difference
  statement. SPS policy allows a rejected manuscript to be resubmitted only once (except scope).
- Conference extension: provide citation and explain the additional contribution.

## Charges and rights

- Page charges: voluntary **$110/page** for the first 10 pages; mandatory **$220/page** above 10
  published pages (subscription route; verify current rates).
- Hybrid subscription route does not require an APC; open access is optional.
- IEEE copyright eCF is completed after acceptance; supplemental material ≤6 pages.

## Submission mechanics

- IEEE Author Portal (`iee.atyponrex.com/journal/tifs-ieee`) for initial submission; follow-up peer
  review may use ScholarOne Manuscripts.
- Main Document - LaTeX requires a zip with `main.tex` at the root; Main Document - PDF requires the
  compiled PDF.
- Cover letter/comments are optional but recommended.
- Build and inspect the portal Reviewer PDF before final submit.
